Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end Customer Churn Analysis Power BI Dashboard designed to evaluate customer behavior, identify primary drivers of attrition, and help businesses proactively improve customer retention.

The dashboard processes key metrics across 6,687 customer accounts, examining demographic data, contract categories, payment methods, extra usage charges, and support call history.

Dashboard Structure & Key Pages
Overview Dashboard

KPI Cards: Overall Churn Rate (26.86%), Total Customers (6,687), and Total Churned (1,796).

Churn Category & Reasons: Breakdowns showing 44.82% of churn attributed to competitors, followed by support attitude, dissatisfaction, and price.

Geographic Distribution: Interactive Bing Map displaying regional churn intensity across North America.

Extra Charges & Data Consumption

Evaluation of Avg Extra Data Charges ($3.37) and Avg Extra International Charges ($33.64).

Churn rate comparisons across data plan categories (e.g., Less than 5 GB, Between 5 and 10 GB, and 10 or more GB).

Insights & Customer Support Analysis

Correlation between Customer Service Calls (6,123 total calls) and customer churn labels.

Comparative analysis showing higher average service calls among churned customers across various states.

Age Groups, Payments & Contracts

Demographics: Churn rate distribution across binned age brackets (noting increased churn risk in older age groups).

Contract Types: Comparison of account lengths (in months) vs. churn rate across Month-to-Month vs. Yearly payment categories.

Tools & Technologies
Business Intelligence Tool: Power BI Desktop

Data Modeling: Star Schema, Custom DAX measures, Binned Groupings

Visual Components: Line & Clustered Column Charts, Decomposition/Donut Charts, GIS Map Integration, Custom Slicers (Account Length Range)
